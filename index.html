About

I’ve led commercial teams of up to 15 across Sydney, Melbourne and offshore markets in Asia and Europe, owning the revenue line from first contact through to renewal. I work in English and Portuguese, and speak conversational Spanish.

Much of that record was built in international education, which is about as complicated as commercial markets get. A single deal can span an institution, an agent, a regulator and the person actually paying. What I took from it is disciplined pipeline governance and partner relationships that hold up under pressure.

I’m now focused on Head of Sales, Customer Experience and Business Development roles outside education.
